# Day 46 - Incident Management

## Incident

An event impacting customers.

Examples:

- Website Down
- Payment Failure

## Alert vs Incident

Alert:
Warning

Incident:
Customer Impact

## Severity Levels

SEV-1
Critical

SEV-2
Major

SEV-3
Medium

SEV-4
Minor

## Incident Lifecycle

Monitoring
↓
Alert
↓
Incident
↓
Investigation
↓
Mitigation
↓
Recovery
↓
RCA
↓
Postmortem

## Key Learning

Incidents require structured response and investigation.
