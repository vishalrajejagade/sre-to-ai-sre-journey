## Concepts Learned

### AIOps

Using automation and AI to assist IT operations.

### Alert Correlation

Grouping related alerts into one incident.

### Root Cause Analysis

Finding the most likely source of failure.

### AI Output Validation

Validating recommendations with evidence.

### Fact vs Assumption

Fact:
Backend connection refused.

Assumption:
Restarting backend will fix it.

### Human Approval

AI recommends.
Humans decide.
